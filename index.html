01Where it begins

Every Libo book starts with a moment like these.

Not the big milestones people celebrate. The quiet, heavy ones that arrive without warning and change the shape of an ordinary week.

  • i.The thoughts that won't settle at three in the morning.
  • ii.The body that suddenly feels like someone else's.
  • iii.The prayer you want to say and can't find the words for.
  • iv.The news you are still taking in, days later.
  • v.The calm you used to have and can't seem to find.

That is where our books begin: right there, with you.

Why Libo exists

A book can't change your season. It can keep you company through it.

Hard seasons are lonely in a particular way. Advice is everywhere, and most of it asks you to be further along than you are. We wanted to make the opposite: books that meet you where you are, give you one small thing to do today, and let you go at your own pace.

That's the whole idea behind Libo. Not to fix anyone. To make sure nobody has to sit with a hard season holding nothing but their phone.

02Life · Book · Print

Our name is our promise, in three words.

Life

It starts with real life.

Not the tidy version. The tired mornings, the waiting rooms, the questions you only ask yourself when the house is quiet. Every book begins with a real season and the people living through it.

Book

A book is patient.

It waits on the nightstand until you're ready. It doesn't send notifications, keep score or ask you to keep a streak. You can close it for a week and pick it up exactly where you left off.

Print

Paper you can make your own.

Underline it, fold the corners, write in the margins. What you write by hand stays on a page you can return to whenever you want to see how far you've come.

03In your hands

What it feels like to open a Libo book.

Whatever the imprint or the subject, our books are built the same way: around you, your energy that day, and your own words.

Room for your words

Lines, boxes and open space to think on paper. Your answers become part of the book.

One small step today

Short practices sized for a hard day. Something you can actually do with the energy you have.

Your own pace

No streaks to break and no score to keep. Miss a day or a month; the book will still be there.

Something new each chapter

Write, trace, map, sort or plan. We vary the practices from chapter to chapter instead of repeating the same page.

06Our promise to every reader

When you're struggling, you deserve the truth and a little care.

Someone who picks up one of our books is often tired, worried or hurting. That is exactly why every book, in every imprint, keeps the same three promises.

We tell you the truth

We check studies, figures and quotations against their original sources before they go to print, and we leave out what we can't verify. No invented experts, no easy miracles.

We make it for you

We design the exercises and drawings for each title and vary them from chapter to chapter. Small things to do, sized for a hard day.

We know our place

We describe only what is inside the book. Our books on health and feelings can sit beside care from a doctor or counselor; they never replace it.

Wherever you are in your season, there is a page here with room for you.
